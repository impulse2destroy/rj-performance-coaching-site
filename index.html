02  Standards

Three things we hold to.

Simple by design. Hard to keep. That's the point.

I.

Train with progression

Every session has a purpose. Load, reps and technique move forward in measured steps, and the log proves it.

II.

Eat with intent

Targets are set once, then adjusted on evidence. Protein first. Real food. Meals you can repeat without resentment.

III.

Review without excuses

Weekly check-ins look at the numbers, not the mood. What worked stays. What didn't changes.
